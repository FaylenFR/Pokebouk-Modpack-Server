# Pokebouk-Modpack-Server
Welcome to Cobblemon Academy 2.0 PokéBouk – Customized and Enhanced Version This modpack is a tailored overhaul of the original Cobblemon Academy 2.0, specially designed to deliver a smooth, immersive multiplayer experience perfectly suited to our community. Important Note: This is a fork of the base pack. I am not the original creator of Cobblemon Academy 2.0; I've simply taken it and customized it to match our vision and server goals. Why this alternative version? The main focus is to make daily multiplayer life more enjoyable while building a genuine, living social and economic system on the server. Economy & Progression

Currency: Implementation of CobbleDollars. NPC Merchants: Easy NPC allows the creation of custom shopkeepers, quest givers, and real interactive dialogues. Balance Changes: Shiny/HA/IV fossils are now available in the shop, some items can no longer be crafted or have significantly reduced spawn rates.

Improvements & Quality of Life

All Mega Evolutions are now available Trials have been optimized Addition of the GTS (Global Trade System) Integration of MegaShowdown features Full adjustment of all fossils Clear lag Clear item Addition of purchasable items in the shop Card gradation system Chimeras and Paradox Pokémon are available Optimization of baits and snacks Unique spawns Addition of breeding system Randomized Pokémon sizes Immersive background music for a deeper experience

Mods Added in This Version:

Numismatic Overhaul Easy NPC → for lively NPCs and custom dialogues Cobblemon Quest FTB Quests / Teams / Library Mega Showdown Daycareplus Cobblegacha Cobbledollar

Want to join us? Just copy the latest link from the Release page and paste to Prism Launcher (Add an Instance and Import) . Everything is already pre-configured (config folders + moddata) to run perfectly on our server.
